YI.WANG V5 original fictional environment assets

world-day.webp / world-night.webp: registered daytime and repainted night landscape.
winter-day.webp / winter-night.webp: matching snow-covered seasonal landscape.
clouds.webp / foreground.webp / window.webp: transparent painted overlay layers.
winter-foreground.webp: RGB snow-painting overlay, clipped in CSS with the original foreground.webp alpha silhouette. It is not a standalone transparent asset.
cat-sleeping.webp: original transparent sleeping cat, anchored to a fixed world position.
social-preview.jpg: daytime painting encoded as JPEG for social sharing.
favicon.svg / apple-touch-icon.png: original yi wordmark icons.

Full generation prompts and provenance: ../ASSET-PROMPTS.md.
All landscape, weather, seasons and cat are fictional environmental artwork.
No real personal photos, locations, biography, ownership of a pet or actual local weather are represented.
User-supplied photos can be added here and referenced in PHOTOS in ../script.js.
Audio is generated locally by Web Audio after opt-in; there are no audio files.
